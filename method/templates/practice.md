## 1. <Short title>

**Concepts:** ana1/limits · **Difficulty:** 2 of 3

Show that aₙ = (2n + 1)/n converges, and find its limit.

<details><summary>Worked solution</summary>

1. Rewrite: aₙ = 2 + 1/n.
2. 1/n → 0, so aₙ → 2.
3. With ε: |aₙ − 2| = 1/n < ε once n > 1/ε.

**Why this works:** splitting off the constant turns it into a known limit.
</details>
